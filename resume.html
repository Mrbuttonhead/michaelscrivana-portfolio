AI as a Creative Medium

I don't just use AI — I build with it. I designed and built a node-based generative pipeline (React Flow) I coded with Claude and wired to Gemini Nano Banana for image generation — because designers don't think in prompts, they think in steps, so I built the tool around composition: reference in, brand-rules check, generate, channel-fit, output. It's produced real channel work across digital and print. Beyond it, I explore generatively across product visualization, storyboarding, and micro-cellular science visuals, and taught the practice to my team in a talk, "AI for Designers." AI is just the newest material on my bench. See it ↗
